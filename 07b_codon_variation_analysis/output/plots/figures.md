## Selection Distribution

**Figure.** Distribution of selection pressure in operon versus core genes. Boxplots show the distribution of normalized pN/pS ratios (npN/pS) for the fructoselysine/glucoselysine operon genes (n=7, blue) and core genes (n=1,251, orange) from 8,596 *Enterococcus faecalis* genomes. Individual gene values are shown as jittered points overlaying the boxplots. The y-axis uses a log10 scale to accommodate the wide range of values. Annotated genes represent the three most conserved core genes showing strong purifying selection (tuf, rpsZ, recA; green labels) and three least conserved core genes showing relaxed or positive selection (gph_2, manX_2, dppE_7; red labels). The pN/pS ratio is calculated as the ratio of non-synonymous to synonymous substitutions within variable codon positions, then normalized by dividing by 2.5 (the expected neutral ratio accounting for the natural 2.5:1 bias of non-synonymous to synonymous sites in the genetic code). Normalized values (npN/pS) <1 indicate purifying selection and values >1 indicate relaxed constraint or positive selection. Operon genes display intermediate selection pressure (npN/pS range: 0.5-3.5), while core genes show greater heterogeneity spanning three orders of magnitude.

## pN vs pS Scatter Plot

**Figure.** Relationship between non-synonymous and synonymous variation across genes. Scatter plot showing the counts of non-synonymous variable sites (y-axis) versus synonymous variable sites (x-axis) for core genes (n=1,251, orange) and fructoselysine/glucoselysine operon genes (n=7, blue) from 8,596 *Enterococcus faecalis* genomes. Each point represents a single gene, with operon genes labeled and plotted above core genes for visibility. Two reference lines are shown: a black dashed line indicating equal counts of non-synonymous and synonymous sites (1:1 ratio), and a red dashed line representing the neutral expectation (2.5:1 ratio) based on the genetic code's inherent bias. Genes falling below the red line experience purifying selection, while those above show relaxed constraint or positive selection. The operon genes cluster in the lower-middle range of variation (20-80 synonymous sites, 20-60 non-synonymous sites), showing moderate but consistent levels of variation. Core genes display a wider distribution, with some showing very high variation counts (>200 sites) and others showing minimal variation, reflecting their diverse functional constraints and evolutionary pressures.
